# Films, music and streams

## Media Player

Browse your drives and folders with the controller. Videos and music play in MediaDeck's own player (mpv): it remembers where you stopped, has subtitles and audio tracks, playback speed and skipping. Pictures open in the photo viewer. USB sticks and drives show up when you plug them in.

Settings → **Media Player**: show all files or only media, only USB drives, hide drives, sort order, thumbnails and folder pictures. Settings → **Playback** and **Subtitles & audio**: skip steps, resume, when a video counts as watched, preferred subtitle and audio languages.

## YouTube

YouTube's own TV interface with [TizenTube](https://github.com/reisxd/TizenTube): no ads, SponsorBlock and DeArrow. The PS / Guide button brings MediaDeck back while it runs.

## Twitch

The [SmartTwitchTV](https://github.com/fgl27/SmartTwitchTV) TV interface; streams play in MediaDeck's player through [Streamlink](https://streamlink.github.io), which leaves Twitch's ads out. Settings → **Twitch** sets the stream quality.

## Apps

Settings → **Apps** installs and updates [PlexDeck](https://github.com/Farathim89/plexdeck) (Plex, opening straight into its TV mode) and [ABS-DA](https://github.com/Farathim89/abs-da) (Audiobookshelf). Any other program (Kodi, Steam, a game …) can be a tile too: the **+** tile in the apps row → *Add a program from this PC…*.
